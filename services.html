Service Scope

Core data analysis services for freelancers, startups, and growing teams

These services cover the most common analytics needs, from preparing messy datasets to delivering dashboards and strategic takeaways.

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Fix raw data quality issues, standardize fields, and prepare reliable datasets for analysis, reporting, and modeling.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Uncover patterns, outliers, correlations, and trends that explain what is happening and where to investigate deeper.

Data Visualization

Create clean visuals that make findings easier to understand for non-technical stakeholders and leadership teams.

Dashboard Development

Build modern Power BI dashboards with KPIs, filters, trend views, and reporting workflows that save time.

SQL Data Analysis

Write efficient queries to extract, join, summarize, and analyze business data for reporting and ad hoc insight needs.

Business Insights

Translate numbers into recommendations that help improve operations, performance, customer understanding, and growth decisions.
